# Tank guide

All new runs start Basic. Select the passive Balanced (shop discount), Vanguard (extra HP/regen), Striker (damage), or Rapid (reload and movement). A passive is independent of the frame. Basic has a single barrel; initial evolutions are free rewards after stage 5. The paid shop always has three slots.

| Stage 5 | Stage 15 | Stage 25 | Preferred cards |
|---|---|---|---|
| Twin | Triplet | Focus, Fan | Damage, reload, penetration |
| Twin | Quad | Crossfire, Quad Guard | Damage, reload, penetration |
| Machine Gun | Minigun | Gatling, Suppressor | Damage, reload, caliber |
| Machine Gun | Destroyer | Annihilator, Artillery | Damage, reload, penetration |
| Sniper | Assassin | Ranger, Marksman | Damage, range, penetration |
| Sniper | Overseer | Overlord, Swarm | Drone Power, reload, range |

| Rammer | Fortress | Landmine, Guardian | Ram Damage, Shield Recharge, Regen, Max Health |

Overlord can choose Mothership after the stage 35 boss (eight independent physical drones). Choose Stay Basic at stages 5, 15 and 25 to reach the stage-40 secret choice: Prism Laser (fast piercing pulses, +48 pierce), Dreadnought (eight elite physical drones), Tempest (eight barrels). No extra permanent inventory; evolutions and run cards expire with the run. Mission unlocks and cosmetics remain in the account.

Shielded Rammer contacts deal damage without damaging the player on that hit. The circle indicates cooldown. Rammer has no gun, gains hull and regeneration, and is not immune to projectiles or boss grids. Incompatible purchased upgrades are removed at evolution with a partial coin refund. Generic Multishot and Velocity are retired from new offers but their old IDs are retained for save compatibility.

Alpha.8: Rammer impact shield now deals bounded splash to nearby non-boss enemies, with larger primary impact, hull and regeneration. Projectile resistance grows with tier; Ram Damage cards boost melee frame impact by 7.5% per level. Prism Railgun replaces Prism Laser's display name but retains its saved ID; the straight piercing pulse is much faster and has a long trail, not instantaneous hitscan. Mothership is unchanged. Normal boss HP is modestly reduced; Twin and Machine Gun branch damage raised.

Alpha.10: Overseer (4), Overlord (6), Swarm (8), Mothership (8), Dreadnought (8) use physical triangular drones, serial replacement, owner-relative orbit, auto attack within command range and aim-stick rally. Reload controls replacement cadence; Drone Power/damage scale contact, range extends command reach. Pierce and Caliber are excluded and refunded on migration of existing drone builds. Idle attack uses rotating groups; manual aim commands the whole wing. Drones lose HP on contact and absorb hostile projectiles. Drone pool maximum 64 across eight players.
