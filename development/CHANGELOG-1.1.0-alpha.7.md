# TESSEL 1.1.0-alpha.7 — evolution & ascension (source candidate)

- Preserve the original four starting passive classes; all new runs begin Basic. The first evolution is selected after stage 5, later branch choices at stages 15 and 25. Mothership follows Overlord after stage 35; Basic may remain unevolved to stage 40 for one of three secret frames.
- 33 stable frame templates; up to ten escort emitters. All weapons use existing pooled bullet storage and geometric renderer. Escort emitters auto-target; free-roaming drone AI and a true continuous player laser are not implemented. Prism uses high-speed piercing pulses.
- Separate three-card paid stat shop with family-specific selection bias, capped damage/reload/penetration levels, new regeneration/shield/drone-power cards. Generic Multishot/Velocity are excluded from new offers; incompatible paid levels are cleared at evolution, with 70% of tracked spend returned (legacy saves estimate spend from base price). Boss perk rewards remain at non-evolution milestones.
- Shorter ordinary stages (25s, 4s cleanup); original 50-stage progression. Health base 10 instead of 6, one free repair on cleared stages and +2 max HP on each boss clear, except stage 50. Shop healing +3. Late enemy health/damage scales; coin drop inflation 10.5% per stage, stage reward performance cap tightened. Rammer frames gain additional hull, impact damage, contact shield, regeneration and projectile resistance.
- Six new enemy definitions: Stalker, Sentinel, Shredder, Ember, Venom, Frost; the last three add stationary fire trails, damage-over-time and short freeze. Existing elite traits and Siphon preserved. Stage 30 has two Pendulums; both must be killed, with reduced simultaneous fire rates.
- Core keeps its grid strikes and two-form borrowed-boss encounter; adds rechargeable shield, telegraphed homing trail with bounded lingering segments, and temporary invulnerable reinforcement phases. Final-arena steady spawn pressure and repeated-boss HP multipliers were reduced after the simulation found that crowd attrition was preventing even skilled agents from beating the Core. Siphon is no longer eligible for Core perk theft. Status timers and Core hazard points travel in compact snapshots.
- Save format 7 reads previous versions. Wire version 21, incompatible with earlier peers. No new APK; no actual device, NFC, or network latency claims from headless simulation.
