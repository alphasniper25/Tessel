# TESSEL alpha.13 — constellation skill map (source-only candidate)
- The owner's reference is a large linked node map, rather than a list of rank buttons. 60 purchasable nodes, 6 sectors, 6 cross-sector bridges, zoom 100–550% with drag, pinch, plus/minus and Fit. Selecting a node shows exact effects, cost, locked/available status and adjacent nodes.
- Run-local coin purchases are optional, not an automatic pop-up or free milestone. First purchase after stage 3; gates at 7, 13, 17, 23 and 35. Fixed costs based on the unlock stage so changing paths later is not penalized. Same wallet as the normal shop; no account power or separate currency. Home preview and pause inspection only; no purchases outside the inter-stage shop.
- Root paths: Refresh, Fortune, Trade, Hull, Mobility, Firepower. All 60 nodes have actual implemented bonuses; crosslinks allow paths to enter a different sector. Free rerolls max 3, initial epic/legendary odds +11 percentage points at full fortune, trade discounts max 20%, modest capped damage/mobility increases. New rarity weights and discounts are provisional until campaign playtesting. Full tree completion not guaranteed in a normal 50-stage run.
- Purchased legacy skills retain equivalent effects without another charge; old unspent milestone offers are retired. Checkpoint FORMAT=8 (reads 1–7), wire 27; existing application ID and permanent signing identity retained.
- Home menu reordered and displayed Codex name replaced by Arsenal. Existing tank/card details, animations, physical drones, art and sounds untouched.
- Unit regression and source compile only. APK assembly explicitly disabled. No distribution or release.
